## Choosing a topic
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for SEP10 is a year-long project all about making a website that informs the viewer of the current and future innovations in the topic of my choosing.

For my project, I chose the topic of CSI( Crime Scene Investigation ) because I want to understand how investigators solve mysterys/crimes with only evidence. I have always found it interesting how a small clue can help explain what happens during the crime. I want to learn how investigators use fingerprints, DNA , and other types of evidence to identify suspects. or solving cases. This article involves how technology has a huge impact on the job.[Modern CSI tools](https://extension.ucr.edu/features/moderncsitools) Other articles I saved while researching about my topic that I found most useful were: [CSI career guide](https://www.gmercyu.edu/academics/learn/crime-scene-investigator-career-guide),[CSI gov career](https://www.cia.gov/careers/ ),[CSI colleges](https://www.forensicscolleges.com/programs/crime-scene-investigation) Im also fascinated in how technology has changed the way crimes are being solved now days. You can just swipe a piece of evidence and technology helps you in so many ways. By researching this topic, I hope to discover new discovery of forensic science and tools investigators use. CSI connects to so many parts of other topics like forensics science, normal science, technology, and problem-solving, which is such an interesting subject to explore deeper in. Im looking forward to learning more about how  investigators use peices of evidence to solve a case .
